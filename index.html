The Revolution: Label Padhega India

5 Rules to Read Any Food Label in 10 Seconds

Founded by Revant Himatsingka (@foodpharmer), the "Label Padhega India" movement empowers 1.4 billion people to hold FMCG food giants accountable. Never let front-of-pack marketing deceive you again.

1

Check the First 3 Ingredients

By law (FSSAI/FDA), ingredients MUST be listed in descending order of weight. The top 3 make up 70% to 90% of what is inside the pack!

2

Spot the Palm Oil Disguise

Companies rarely write "Palm Oil". Look for: Palmolein, Fractionated Palm Oil, Palm Kernel Oil, Hydrogenated Vegetable Fat.

3

Unmask the 40+ Names of Sugar

Brands split sugar into 3-4 different aliases: Maltodextrin, Invert Sugar, Liquid Glucose, Dextrose, Cereal Extract.

4

Beware of "Zero Maida" Tricks

"Made with 100% Atta" on the front may contain 98% maida and just 2% whole wheat atta! Always look for the percentage in parentheses.

5

The Serving Size Illusion

Brands shrink the serving size to a tiny 10g so sugar appears low. Always check the per 100g nutrition column!

✓

The Golden Clean Rule

If your grandmother would not recognize an ingredient in her kitchen, you are eating an industrial chemical formulation, not real food.
